Privacy & Cryptographic Custody Policy

BIP-39 COMPLIANT

1. Self-Custody and Zero-Knowledge Architecture: Cold Wallet operates on non-custodial self-custodial principles. Your 12-word BIP-39 Secret Recovery Phrase and ECDSA private keys are generated and held exclusively on client side. They are never transmitted, logged, or accessible by our servers.

2. Data Collection: Only anonymous public addresses and transaction intents are processed for execution across decentralized networks and RPC nodes. No personal identification data (PII) is tracked.

3. Regulatory Compliance: Transaction records committed to the immutable ledger adhere to standard decentralized accounting protocols.
